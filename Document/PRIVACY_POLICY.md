## 1. Information we collect

**a) Information you give us**
- Your **name** and **time zone** (during setup).
- **Meeting and task details** you create: titles, dates, times, durations, notes, reminders, to-do items.
- **Details of people you schedule with** (an attendee's name, email, and/or phone number) when you ask us to invite or remind them.
- The **content of messages** you send to the bot (text and voice notes).

**b) Information from WhatsApp / Meta**
- Your **WhatsApp phone number** (your identifier) and the **messages you send** to our number, delivered to us through the WhatsApp Business Platform (operated by Meta). Your use of WhatsApp is also governed by [WhatsApp's Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).

**c) Information from Google (only if you connect your Google account)**
With your explicit OAuth consent, Araka accesses the following Google data to provide its features:
| Google scope | What we access | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Calendar | Your events | Create meetings, check for conflicts, cancel/reschedule |
| Google Meet | (via Calendar) | Generate meeting links |
| Google Contacts (read-only) | Names, emails, phones | Resolve "schedule with [name]" to the right person |
| Gmail (read-only) | Message metadata & content of emails **you ask about** | Answer questions like "did X email me?" |
| Google Sheets | A to-do sheet we create | Store your to-do list |
| Profile email | Your Google email | Identify the connected account |

**d) Information collected automatically**
- Technical operational data: message IDs (to avoid processing duplicates), timestamps, consent status, and limited server logs (for security and debugging).

We do **not** collect payment card data, government IDs, location, or special-category data, and we ask you not to send sensitive personal data (e.g., health, financial, biometric) to the bot.

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information **only** to provide and operate Araka:
- Create, view, change, cancel, and remind you about meetings, tasks, and reminders.
- Resolve a contact's details so we can invite the right person.
- Check your calendar for conflicts before booking.
- Send you confirmations and reminders, and — where you ask us to involve another person — send that person a **WhatsApp template message** about the meeting (they can reply **STOP** to opt out at any time).
- Answer your questions about your own calendar and email.
- Operate, secure, debug, and improve the service.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, and we do **not** sell it.

## 3. How AI is used

Araka uses AI **only** to understand your request and produce a helpful reply:
- **Sarvam AI** (sarvam.ai) processes the text of your message — and, when your request requires it, the specific calendar event or email you asked about — to understand intent and draft a response.
- **Groq** (groq.com) transcribes your voice notes to text.

This processing happens **solely to fulfil your request in real time**. Your data, including any Google data, is **not used to train our models or our AI providers' models**, is **not** used for advertising, and is **not** sold or transferred to third parties for their own purposes.

## 4. Google user data — Limited Use (required disclosure)

Araka's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its **Limited Use** requirements. Specifically:
- We use Google user data **only** to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
- We do **not** transfer this data to others except as necessary to provide or improve those features, for security, or to comply with law.
- We do **not** use Google user data for advertising.
- We do **not** allow humans to read your Google data, **except**: (a) with your explicit consent; (b) for security purposes (e.g., investigating abuse); (c) to comply with applicable law; or (d) where the data is aggregated and anonymized for operations.
- We do **not** use Google user data to develop, improve, or train **generalized/non-personalized** AI and/or ML models.

You can revoke Araka's access at any time by sending **disconnect** to the bot, or via your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 5. Who we share information with (sub-processors)

We share information only with the service providers needed to run Araka:
| Provider | Purpose | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform)** | Message delivery | Global |
| **Google LLC** | Your Calendar / Meet / Contacts / Gmail / Sheets | Global |
| **Sarvam AI** | Natural-language understanding & replies | India / global |
| **Groq** | Voice transcription | Global (US) |
| **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure** | Application & database hosting | [REGION, e.g. India/Hyderabad] |

The **person you schedule a meeting with** will receive a WhatsApp message from our number about that meeting; we share only what is needed (the booker's name, meeting title, time, and link). We do not otherwise disclose your information, and we never sell it.

## 6. Data retention

- We keep your data while your account is active.
- You can delete everything at any time by sending **delete my data** to the bot — this erases your meetings, reminders, notes, to-dos, and your Google connection.
- Encrypted database **backups** are retained for up to **14 days** and then deleted.
- Limited security/operational logs are retained for a short period.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption of your stored Google access tokens, restricted server access, the application bound to a private port behind a reverse proxy, and automatic security updates. Google OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and, where applicable, the EU/UK **GDPR**), you may have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you,
- **Correct** inaccurate data,
- **Erase** your data (send **delete my data**),
- **Withdraw consent** (send **disconnect** for Google; **STOP** to end reminders),
- **Grievance redressal** — contact our Grievance Officer below.

To exercise any right, message the bot or email **team@forzasport.in**. We respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

## 9. Children

Araka is not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under **18**. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than yours (e.g., by Google, Groq, or Sarvam). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
