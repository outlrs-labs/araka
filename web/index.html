● How It Works

Three messages.
Meeting booked for both of you.

No apps to install, no dashboards to learn. Just text Araka like you'd text a friend.

1

Text naturally

"Set up a call with Priya tomorrow at 3" — Araka understands natural language, resolves contacts from Google, and checks your calendar for conflicts.

2

Confirm with a tap

Araka shows you the details and waits for your explicit confirmation. It never books, cancels, or invents a time unilaterally. You're always in control.

3

Both sides notified

A Google Meet is created, and the other person gets a WhatsApp confirmation too — with their own reminders. Both of you show up. That's it.
